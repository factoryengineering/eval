### 1.4 Findings
- **Cascade masking.** The scenario buckets (`read`, `replace`, `delete`, `race`, `create`) fail at levels 1-2 with the create bootstrap exactly as in the idempotent-writes home (create fails in 100 of 100 compiled level 1 trials; at level 2 create passed in 16 of 90 and the scenario buckets still fail there). They carry no independent information at levels 1-2. The **static, lint and spec buckets do not depend on the bootstrap** (they inspect the code and the OpenAPI document), so their level 1-2 rates are real.
- **(i) Skill-line candidates, independent of the bootstrap (level 1 or 2 signal, n = 21):** `lint write-ops-declare-428`, `write-ops-require-if-match`, `put-declares-412-and-404`, `read-declares-304-404-410`, `create-declares-201-200-409`, `put-and-delete-declare-no-conflicting-gaps` (5 of 5 models, about 95 % of trials), `static etag-header-set`, `if-match-required`, `internal-key-int-hidden`, `problem-details-used`, `sha256-used-for-etag` (5 of 5), `spec spec-declares-observed-statuses` (5 of 5), `static cancellation-token-passed` (haiku, sonnet, swe-2, flash at level 1: 8, 15, 21, 17 of 21; luna 3/21 quiet), `lint no-server-owned-fields-in-schemas` (haiku, luna, swe-2, flash; sonnet 0/21), `static no-entity-in-responses` (luna 8/21 and flash 11/21 at level 1; haiku, sonnet 0), `lint request-bodies-required` (sonnet 7/21 at level 1, 9/21 at level 2: it uses `[FromBody] T?`), `spec spec-response-bodies-match-schemas` (level 2: haiku, sonnet, luna, flash; sonnet 14/21 compiled).
- **(ii) Never fail anywhere (regression monitors):** `lint delete-no-404`, `lint ids-format-uuid`, `lint no-int-id-params`, `lint operation-ids-unique`, `static no-int-id-in-dtos`, `static routes-use-guid`. Almost nothing at level 3 fails the static/lint/spec buckets, so the full spec is followed on most of them.
- **(iii) Level 3.** Compile: same as in idempotent-writes (L3 25 compile failures of 99 answers plus 3 host failures). Pass of every graded check: haiku 3/21, sonnet 8/21, luna 0/21, swe-2 0/21, flash 0/12. Signals at level 3: `spec spec-declares-observed-statuses` (all five, 9, 5, 7, 13, 3 failing trials), `lint write-ops-require-if-match` (sonnet 4/21, swe-2 12/21, flash 4/12), `scenario replace` (luna 8, swe-2 14, flash 3/12), `scenario delete` (luna 7, swe-2 13), `lint routes-cover-descriptor` (haiku 9/21, sonnet 5/21), `lint request-bodies-required` (swe-2 6/21), `scenario race` (swe-2 8/21). Transcripts read:
  1. `spec-declares-observed-statuses` and the replace/delete scenarios: the 400-instead-of-428 trap (declared `[Required]` header plus `[ApiController]`): the observed 400 is not declared in the document (luna 7, swe-2 13 trials: the single violation is `DELETE ... -> 400 ... One or more validation errors`). Genuine model behaviour.
  2. `routes-cover-descriptor` (haiku 9, sonnet 5): the OpenAPI document contains no operations at all because the model left off `[ApiController]` (haiku wrote "because its automatic 400 would pre-empt the 428"); controllers without it are not in the ApiExplorer document. Genuine, and linked to the same trap.
  3. `write-ops-require-if-match`: `[FromHeader(Name = "If-Match")] string ifMatch` without `[Required]` is not reported required in the generated document (checked with a throwaway test on the blog reference). The prompt asks for a required header. Genuine; ASP.NET Core platform fact.
  4. **Oracle defects found and fixed during smoke** (static helper blindness, entity-argument false positives, interleaver deadlock under app-level locks) had been inflating exactly these static and race buckets; they are fixed in the harness before this run, so these level 3 rates are after the fix. No further oracle bug was found by reading level 3 failures in this home.
  5. Prompt gaps found after the baseline and **not** applied (see idempotent-writes 1.4 (iii) item 3): racing identical PUTs and the ETag header on no-op PUT 204. They affect scenario buckets (`replace`, `race`) only.
- **(iv) Domain concentration:** `no-server-owned-fields-in-schemas` at level 1 is concentrated on blog and support (haiku 5/2/0, swe-2 4/6/0, luna 5/2/3, flash 4/5/3, sonnet 0): the `Views` and `ViewCount` fields leak into responses more often than `DownloadCount`/`LastAuditAt`. A swe-2 logistics-naked transcript shows a response DTO that simply omits both fields, so this is model/prompt-wording behaviour (the field names read differently), not an oracle defect. `routes-cover-descriptor` is concentrated on blog and logistics (the two parent domains) for haiku (4/1/4) because those use the shared `ApiControllerBase` without `[ApiController]`. Other level 3 concentrations are compile artefacts as in idempotent-writes.
- Limits: shared completions (not an independent replication); about 31 buckets x 5 models x 3 levels tested at alpha 0.05 (multiple comparisons); 7 trials per cell; swe-2-flash level 3 n = 12.

Pursue first (candidates only): the 428/`If-Match` trap (spec/replace/delete/lint, 5 buckets), `cancellation-token-passed`, `request-bodies-required`/`write-ops-require-if-match` (nullable and `[Required]` mechanics), `no-entity-in-responses` and `no-server-owned-fields-in-schemas`.
